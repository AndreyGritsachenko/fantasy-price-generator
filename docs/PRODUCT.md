\# Fantasy Price Generator



\## Goal



Create a reusable fantasy goods and price generation system.



The system should allow users to:



\- search goods;

\- browse goods by category;

\- calculate contextual prices;

\- modify prices based on environment;

\- modify prices based on merchant attitude;

\- add new goods;

\- add groups of goods;

\- manage categories;

\- manage price modifiers.



\## Applications



The business logic will later be reused by:



\- desktop application;

\- web application;

\- mobile application.



The first phase does not contain UI.



\## Price generation



A product has a base price.



The final price may be affected by:



\- environment;

\- location;

\- product availability;

\- merchant attitude;

\- additional modifiers.



Exact formulas may evolve during development.
